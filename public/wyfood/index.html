About WY Food

A source-verified guide to farmers markets and food events within reach of West Yorkshire. Built so you can answer "is there a market this weekend?" in two taps, without trawling half a dozen organiser websites.

Schedules still change, though. Always confirm before travelling — every market has a "Check dates" button that links straight to the organiser's site.

This is a personal project, not an official directory. Spotted something wrong, or know a market or festival that should be on here? Drop a line via Contact.

A note on privacy: when you enter a postcode, it's looked up via postcodes.io, a free public-data service. The site itself has no analytics or tracking.
